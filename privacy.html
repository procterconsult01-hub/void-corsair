Privacy Policy — Void Corsair

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Void Corsair ("the game") is published by Austine Agbaghara.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your device only: doubloon balance, ship upgrades, sound setting, captain name, and your best scores. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to those transactions: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
